Places to Explore in Visakhapatnam

Visakhapatnam has a mix of beaches, natural attractions, museums, and historical locations. Explore some of the city's popular destinations below.

Place 1 — RK Beach

Category: Beach

Location: Beach Road, Visakhapatnam

RK Beach is a popular coastal destination in the city. Visitors can enjoy the sea view, walk along the beach road, and experience the lively atmosphere.

Learn More

Place 2 — Kailasagiri

Category: Nature & Viewpoint

Location: Hilltop, Visakhapatnam

Kailasagiri is a hilltop park surrounded by greenery. It provides scenic views of the city and coastline.

Learn More

Place 3 — INS Kurusura Submarine Museum

Category: Museum

Location: RK Beach Road

The INS Kurusura Submarine Museum is a unique attraction where visitors can learn about submarine operations and naval history.

Learn More

Place 4 — Simhachalam Temple

Category: Heritage & Culture

Location: Simhachalam

Simhachalam is a historic temple known for its architecture, cultural importance, and religious heritage.

Learn More

Place 5 — Yarada Beach

Category: Beach & Nature

Location: Yarada

Yarada Beach is surrounded by hills and offers a quieter coastal experience away from the busy parts of the city.

Learn More

Place 6 — Indira Gandhi Zoological Park

Category: Wildlife & Nature

Location: Visakhapatnam

The zoological park is a large green space where visitors can learn about different species of animals and birds.
