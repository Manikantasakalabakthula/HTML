Welcome to City Explorer, your simple guide to discovering the beautiful city of Visakhapatnam. Explore beaches, hills, historical places, parks, food, and other interesting attractions around the city.

Featured Places

1. RK Beach

Category: Beach

RK Beach is one of the most popular attractions in Visakhapatnam. It is a great place to enjoy the sea, take a walk, and experience the city's coastal atmosphere.

2. Kailasagiri

Category: Nature & Viewpoint

Kailasagiri is a hilltop park offering beautiful views of Visakhapatnam and the Bay of Bengal. It is known for its greenery, viewpoints, and peaceful surroundings.

Category: Museum
