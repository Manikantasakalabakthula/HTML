Kailasagiri — A Hilltop View of Visakhapatnam

Kailasagiri is one of Visakhapatnam's well-known hilltop attractions. The park is surrounded by greenery and offers views of the city and the Bay of Bengal.

About Kailasagiri

Kailasagiri is located on a hill overlooking the coastline. The area is known for its scenic surroundings, walking areas, viewpoints, and large statues. It is a popular place for visitors who want to enjoy nature and views of the city.
